D-030 (Layer-2 inversion channel, hardened): SD-VAE round-trip error probe registered as 10th channel. V1 (clean-trained) had 26.7% FPR on wild reals and cost ffpp -0.0196 — compressed reals reconstruct well and read as fake. Fix: laundering-augmented training (JPEG q50 + q30/0.6x views of reals AND fakes + ffpp faces) cut FPR to 11.3% and flipped ffpp delta to +0.0036. p6v7 promoted (all 5 gates PASS, flux +0.0113); zero-shot: FLUX 0.956, MJ 0.870, SG2 coverage consciously traded to face/real-dist probes. Law: any generator-agnostic channel MUST be trained on laundered views or it poisons compressed domains.
D-031 (Layer-1 VLM as detector: NEGATIVE RESULT, measured and closed): a local 7B VLM (Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct-4bit, MLX) interrogated with a fixed question bank is NOT a discriminative deepfake channel. Measured on 40 COCO reals vs 60 fakes (SD1.5+CDF): physics/anomaly bank AUC 0.5000 (never emits "inconsistent" — 0% flags on BOTH classes); forensic/leading-question bank AUC 0.3500 (flags "waxy skin / warped background" on 100% of CAMERA reals — leading questions manufacture anomalies). Two failure modes: silence and hypochondria; nothing in between at this model scale. Decision: the VLM never votes in court. Repurposed as the explanation engine (deepfake/api/vlm_explain.py, /api/explain endpoint): neutral, non-leading scene narration attached to an already-rendered verdict — no evidence space, no restack needed, hash-keyed answer cache (data/caches/vlm_explain.jsonl), ~7s cold / instant cached. Do not re-litigate VLM-as-detector without a materially larger model or grounded tooling.
D-036 (r8 self-generated wild-style audio diet: FAILED — documented dead end, reverted to p4+coverage gate): hypothesis: retrain the audio probe on a self-generated wild-style diet (ASVspoof bonafide + wildized views via numpy DSP chain [noise/clip/bandpass/resample/quantize/EQ], ASVspoof multi-attack spoofs + 350 fresh Kokoro-82M TTS clips, all identically wildized; balanced 2160 train vectors). Result: r8 val AUC 0.909 (discriminates its own distribution fine) but EXTERNALLY: DF-Eval AUC 0.5615 (unchanged vs p4 0.5593), FPR@0.5 WORSENED 0.795→0.9165, ASVspoof 2021 LA INVERTED (AUC 0.3321), and the D-035 coverage gate was neutralized (wild data absorbed into the broader manifold, abstention 84%→1.85%). Mechanism: synthetic degradation carries no class information — both heads get the same wildizer, so the probe learns nothing transferable about real-world deception; it just shifts its boundary toward 'degraded-sounding = fake', worsening FPR on genuinely wild reals. Lesson: the wild fake distribution cannot be approximated by single-engine TTS (Kokoro) + synthetic DSP; real in-the-wild training audio is irreplaceable. REVERTED: AUDIO_PROBE back to p4 + D-035 coverage gate (verified: abstention 0.839 restored, external metrics identical to D-035 baseline). The path to a trustworthy audio branch requires a REAL in-the-wild training corpus — ASVspoof 5 (EULA registration) or the In-the-Wild dataset (Müller et al., request form at deepfake-total.com) — both need human action; Diet + extractor scripts kept (build_audio_r8_diet.py, extract_audio_r8.py, artifacts/p8_audio kept for reference) for when such a corpus lands.

D-033 (External validation, audio: Deepfake-Eval-2024 FAILS the audio branch — the exact failure mode D-028 predicted, now measured): ran the deployed audio probe over the official Deepfake-Eval-2024 test protocol (arXiv 2503.02857; 703 matched in-the-wild audio files: 395 Real / 308 Fake; evaluation-only use per dataset terms). Result: AUC 0.5593 (near chance), TPR@0.5 0.877 but FPR@0.5 0.795 — the branch trained on lab TTS (ASVspoof LA, EER 3.5% in-domain) flags ~80% of REAL social-media audio as fake. This mirrors the benchmark paper's finding that open-source SOTA audio drops ~48% AUC in the wild, and confirms the audio branch must be treated as UNTRUSTED for real-world audio until retrained on modern in-the-wild-style fakes (ASVspoof 5 / 2024-era TTS corpus → restack r8) — Deepfake-Eval-2024 data itself is evaluation-only and must never enter training.
